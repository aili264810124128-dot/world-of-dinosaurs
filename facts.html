Dinosaur Discoveries

Explore some interesting discoveries about dinosaurs and fossils!

What Are Fossils?

Fossils are the preserved remains or traces of ancient living things. They help paleontologists understand what life was like millions of years ago.

How Are Fossils Discovered?

Paleontologists search for fossils in places such as deserts, cliffs and rock formations. When they find fossils, they carefully collect and study them.

Interesting Dinosaur Facts

1. Not all dinosaurs were enormous.

2. Some dinosaurs had feathers.

3. Birds are living descendants of dinosaurs.

4. Dinosaurs lived millions of years before humans.

Why Is Paleontology Important?

Paleontology helps us understand the history of life on Earth. By studying fossils, scientists can learn about extinct animals, ancient environments and how living things changed over time.
